## Scientific source and data

**Isabel Moreno-Sánchez, Francesc Font-Clos and Álvaro Corral** wrote *Large-Scale Analysis of Zipf's Law in English Texts* (2016), PLOS ONE 11(1): e0147073, DOI [10.1371/journal.pone.0147073](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0147073), and released [the frequency dataset, version 3](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.1515919.v3). The book collection originates in Project Gutenberg; this repository analyzes the authors' released frequency vectors, not a newly scraped Gutenberg corpus.

NumPy, SciPy, mpmath, Matplotlib and Python supplied the numerical and plotting tools; they are dependencies, not bundled copies. Their authors retain their respective credits and licenses. No implementation was copied from another paper-reproduction project; those projects supplied high-level precedent only.

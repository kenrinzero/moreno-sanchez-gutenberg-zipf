- **Notes:** Promoted from the study unit `internal prior-study reference` at the user's request (2026-09-29 screen of the whole `.papers` library). Feasibility was prospected, adversarially re-checked and reconciled (2026-09-30) before filing; facts below are checked unless marked UNCHECKED.

## The idea

*PLoS ONE* 11:e0147073 (2016) fits three one-parameter Zipf laws from n=1 to every English Gutenberg text with L>100 tokens (31,075): f1 (power law in the mass, Hurwitz zeta), f2 (power law in the survival S(n)), f3 (Mandelbrot); ML by Brent on 1<β≤4, discrete KS with 100 refitted MC draws (p. 5). Claims:

1. **Split.** At p0=0.05 f2 fits ~40%, f1 ~15%, both 2.7%; f2:f1 "around 2.6" at every level above the 0.01 MC resolution (p. 7). Table 1 (p. 12): f1-only 3,695 (3,614 / 81 by sign of R12), f2-only 11,486 (120 / 11,366), both 829 (431 / 398), total 16,010. Table 2 (p. 13): significant LR 1,666 for f1, 9,423+3 for f2, none against ML-KS; neither row 510 / 11,431.
2. **Length.** f1 wins below ~13,000 tokens, irrelevant beyond ~10^5; f2 still fits many above 200,000 (p. 8; Fig. 7, p. 12).
3. **β.** 2.03±0.15 (f1), 2.02±0.17 (f2) for p≥0.05 (Fig. 8, p. 13). β falls with L (Figs. 9–10, pp. 14–15); fixed-β synthetic texts do not, so "not an artifact"; genre is proposed but "we cannot test this hypothesis" (p. 11).
4. **Lengths.** Tail exponent of the length pdf 2.92±0.15 (Fig. 1, p. 6).

**Angle.** (a) Reproduce Tables 1–2 on the authors' own vectors, with a per-text MC flip audit. (b) β(L): full-range f2 β is nearly a hapax statistic whose within-book fall with length is published (Bernhardsson et al. 2009; ref. [35]), so measure how much of the across-text trend a within-book finite-size model carries and whether a genre residual survives on Gutenberg category labels. (c) Filter/tokenizer sensitivity on 30,995 books shared with SPGC. (d) A 9,213-book post-2014 holdout.

## Stages

1. **Ingest and deterministic targets (T3).** md5, drop `__MACOSX`, L=Σn; Fig. 1 tail (Deluca–Corral, ref. [34]); Fig. 5 edges; flag odd catalog items. **Done when:** every target matches or its divergence is logged; an L-total mismatch kills the "authors' own data" premise.
2. **Sampler/test validation (T2).** f2 inversion, f1 rejection (C=1.2158 at β=2, p. 16), f3 rejection (C→2); discrete KS; refit per draw. **Done when:** 2,000 null p-values per model at N∈{300, 3,000, 30,000} pass uniformity at 0.01 and β is recovered within 0.005.
3. **Reproduction at 100 draws (T2).** Tables 1–2 incl. zero cells; Figs. 3, 7(a), 8 moments; Fig. 2 guides 0.74/0.78; Fig. 9 edges (f1 4,759/9,153/14,142, max 241,358; f2 17,457/41,993/67,588, max 348,992); Fig. 10 on accepted texts; visualizer redraws Figs. 3, 7(a). **Done when:** every cell is inside its pre-registered band or has forensics open.
4. **MC audit (T2).** Second 100-draw pass → text-level κ; sequential MC to ≥10^4 draws for p in [0.01, 0.2]; tables and ratio at p0=0.01…0.5; ≥ vs >. **Done when:** each text's 0.05 status has MC SE ≤0.005 (or is flagged) and the flip table is published.
5. **β(L): finite size vs residual (T1 design, T2 run).** SPGC books with L≥3×10^4, category-stratified, minus omnibus/compilation/reference IDs and omnibus–single duplicates; list testable categories per L range first. Prefix plus ≥50 windows per L' over 10^3–10^4.5, skipping the first 5% of tokens. Tests: (i) [35]'s scaling law, from each whole book and its V(L'), predicts prefix β_f2(L'); (ii) meta-book: a section matches whole short books of that length and category or author; (iii) the across-text curve (SPGC counts) minus the within-book prediction leaves a residual, fitted on Category and LoCC. **Done when:** the share s of the across-text slope carried within books has a bootstrap CI classified against the pre-registered outcomes, and category effects have CIs.
6. **Filter/tokenizer (T2).** Refit the 30,995 overlap books on SPGC. **Done when:** flip table and Δβ quantiles are published against the Stage-4 MC flip rate.
7. **Holdout (T2).** English SPGC, ID>45,117, L>100; fractions post-stratified by first-LoCC class × L-bin, compared with Stage 6 overlap rates. **Done when:** both are reported with CIs and the write-up says "not a replication".

## Step-0 hooks

- **Ratio vs table.** Table 1: 12,315/4,524 = 2.72; text "around 2.6" (p. 7), "about twice" (p. 14). Pre-register 2.72±0.1 at 0.05 plus the p0 range (subsample 2.53/2.34/3.08 at 0.05/0.2/0.5).
- **Fig. 4(c) is an index.** Caption: Guizot, *Popular History of France* Vol. I, L=2,081. That vector is PG 28879, "A Linked Index to the Project Gutenberg Editions"; PG 11951 (Vol. 1) has L=143,302, f3 β=1.497.
- **[35] backs a stable tail, not a stable full-range β.** p. 11 cites [35] for "the stability of the exponent β … for a single growing text". [35] (NJP 15:093033; `internal prior-study reference` copy, lines 267–271, 375–377, 466–468) shows the *apparent* log-log exponent falling across prefixes, explained by a fixed scaling function whose transition point moves with L; only the tail exponent is stable. The a=1 β is an apparent exponent, so a within-book fall is expected and the fixed-β i.i.d. control tests estimator bias only. [35]'s collapse fails below n≈3 (lines 389–391), where full-range fits live, so Stage 5 test (i) can fail.
- **Stage 5 outcomes** (replacing a "≥50%" prediction that prospecting already implied): (A) CI of s includes 100% → finite size suffices; (B) CI excludes 100% with nonzero category effects → genre residual; B with CI below 50% favours the paper.
- **p convention.** Fig. 7 "p≥p0", Fig. 8 "p≥0.05", Fig. 9 "p>0.05". Subsample: 6/400 f2 p at exactly 0.05; 39.3% (≥) vs 37.8% (>). Pre-register: ≥ reproduces Table 1; > falls ~400–500 short on f2.
- **MC resolution.** SE of a 100-draw p near 0.05 is 0.022. A binomial model on the Fig. 2 p^−0.78 guide predicts ~1,400 f2 flips between runs with the total moving <100, and ~2% fewer accepts with exact p. Pre-register 500–2,000 flips, net within ±4%. Storey π0 (λ=0.5): 0.20 f2, 0.07 f1, descriptive.
- **Bands (before Stage 3).** Table 1 margins ±3%; interior cells ±5% or ±50; Table 2 zeros ≤5; Fig. 9 interior edges ±5%; the two single-text maxima reported, not gated.
- **Fig. 5 edges off the 2,000-text grid** (2026-09-30). Caption (p. 10): 15 bins of 2,000, L1=[115, 5,291], L6=[25,739, 34,378], L13=[89,476, 103,767]. All are Figshare L values, at sorted ranks 1,998 / 9,998–11,999 / 23,999–25,998 (grid 2,000 / 10,001–12,000 / 24,001–26,000); 15×2,000≠31,075. A binning convention or 2–3 extra short texts.
- **Fig. 10.** The last f1 bin "contains a single datapoint" (p. 15), so the population is accepted texts (848 texts have L≥2×10^5). Check that exactly one f1-accepted text falls in that bin.
- **f3 domain.** Eq. 5 is a pmf only for 1<β<2 (for 2<β<3, Γ(2−β)<0 makes f3(n≥2)<0), yet Brent runs to 4. Restrict; count edge fits (13 books: interior, 1.48–1.82).
- **Snapshot.** Accessed July 2014; ID 45,117 issued 2014-03-12, 46,300 on 2014-07-16. Cut the holdout by ID.
- **Passed:** Table sums; Table 2 ≤ Table 1; C=0.6079/0.5=1.2158; min/max L; Fig. 4; Fig. 9 edges exist as L values.

## Seed notes / risks

- **Expected outcome.** Claims 1–2 likely confirm cleanly; the value is in Stages 4–7.
- **Stage 5 narrowed by review.** The within-book fall is prior art. The drafted ≥200 books at L≥2×10^5 could not reach the paper's genres (9 plays, 10 children's, 25 poetry), and that tail is omnibus-heavy (231/847 on a conservative title regex, 415 on the review's) with duplicates (PG 4500, Meredith's *Complete Works*, beside 23 other Meredith items). What stays new: the between-text residual and a quantitative test of [35] on the a=1 fit. Tunnicliffe & Hunter's literature-type ordering is a qualitative cross-check only, outside the oracle (different estimator and range).
- **Ingest traps.** `__MACOSX` twins; no strings; `Issued` not monotone in ID (PG 57383 dated 2009); 80 books lack SPGC counts; indexes and dictionaries. Non-plain items: PG 4656 (*Checkmates for Four Pieces*, L=2,291,973) Dataset, 21805 Sound, 39849 Gaelic, 38580/40597/40986 mixed-language; 28520, 30360, 36169 absent from the catalog. Flag, don't drop.
- **Filter mismatch.** SPGC deletes hyphenated compounds and clitic pieces ("n't", "'s") outright; these skew to hapaxes, which drive full-range fits. Stage 6 measures filter plus tokenizer; Stage 5 takes its across-text curve from SPGC. Random thinning falls faster still (Moby-Dick 2.93 at ~1.1k): a bound only.
- **Labels.** `Category:` shelves are 2026 multi-label metadata; assignment UNCHECKED; cross-check LoCC.
- **Not a replication.** Holdout composition (2026-09-30): LoCC class P 57.8% (Figshare) vs 42.7%, D 8.3% vs 14.0%, Novels 33.7% vs 25.3%. L-reweighting is not enough; compare post-stratified rates only.
- **Topic fatigue.** Third Zipf-family fit; reuse the Axtell member's discrete-MLE/MC machinery.
- **Parked.** Gerlach–Altmann correlation-aware testing (6.4 GB tokens; different null); CJK comparison (zipf dossier covers it); two-parameter tail race; lemmatization (no strings); 2026 re-mirror (24,401 texts from 2014-08-01) only if Stage 7 drifts.
